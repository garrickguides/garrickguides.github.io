Bonus · Chapter 13

Maintenance reminders

Pick your printer and tap the button. Your phone opens the reminders as a calendar file: confirm to add them. They repeat on their own, starting next Saturday.

Before any task: switch the printer off, unplug it and let the nozzle and the bed cool fully. The book has the steps and the lubricant rules for each task.

The A2L, P2S, X2D, H2S, H2C and H2D Pro have no table in the book: follow the printer's own reminders and the wear-part checks in Chapter 13.
